Cam belt replacement Ambergate

The frequency of cam belt replacement varies widely between vehicles. Factors like your car's age, mileage, and specific engine design play a significant role in determining the recommended replacement interval. Consulting your vehicle's owner's manual or a trusted mechanic is essential to ensure you adhere to the correct schedule. Ignoring this crucial maintenance task can lead to catastrophic engine failure, resulting in costly repairs. A broken cam belt can cause valves to collide with pistons, damaging both components. Regular inspections and timely replacements are vital to prevent such costly breakdowns and maintain the longevity of your engine.

Cam belt replacement Stapleford

Timing Belts: Keeping Your Engine in Sync

Timing belts, often called cambelts, are essential engine components that ensure smooth and efficient operation. They synchronise the crankshaft, which powers the pistons, with the camshaft, which controls the flow of fuel into and out of the engine cylinders. This precise timing is crucial, as even minor disruptions can lead to severe engine damage.

Signs of a Failing Timing Belt:

  • Ticking or Clicking Noises: Unusual sounds emanating from the engine could signal timing belt wear.
  • Engine Performance Issues: Rough idling, hesitation, or a decrease in engine power can be symptoms of timing belt problems.
  • Visible Damage: In some cases, visual inspection may reveal cracks, fraying, or missing teeth on the timing belt.

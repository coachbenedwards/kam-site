ABOUT KAM SERVICING

KAM Servicing is a trusted family-run business that has been serving motorists across Derbyshire and the East Midlands for over 30 years.

What began as a small garage in Sawley, Derbyshire has grown into one of the largest independent garage networks in the region. Our continued commitment to quality workmanship, honest advice, and excellent customer service has helped us build long-standing relationships with drivers across the area.

Today, more than three decades later, KAM Servicing operates 16 centres throughout the East Midlands, covering locations from M1 Junction 24 to Junction 28. As we continue to grow, additional centres will be opening over the coming years, allowing us to serve even more customers across the region.

KAM Servicing is proud to be rated Excellent on Trustpilot. We are also accredited members of the Good Garage Scheme and recognised by the AA for warranty repair work. These accreditations reflect our commitment to maintaining the highest standards of service, giving you complete confidence that your vehicle is in safe and professional hands.

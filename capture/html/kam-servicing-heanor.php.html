Cam belt replacement Heanor

A timing belt (or cam belt) is a vital part of your car's engine. If it fails, it can cause serious damage. Watch out for these signs that it might need replacing:

Strange noises: A squealing or slapping sound can indicate a problem.

Reduced power: If your car feels sluggish or lacks acceleration, it could be a symptom.

Check engine light: This light might illuminate if there's an issue with the timing belt.

Timing belts are made of rubber and can wear out over time. It's recommended to replace them at the recommended service intervals. The timing belt tensioner should usually be replaced at the same time.

If you suspect your timing belt is faulty, contact The KAM Servicing for a professional inspection and replacement. Early action can prevent expensive engine repairs and save you money.

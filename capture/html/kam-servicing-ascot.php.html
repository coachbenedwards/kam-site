Cam belt replacement Derby, Ascot Drive

Your timing belt is a critical component of your car's engine, but it's often overlooked. A worn or damaged timing belt can lead to serious engine problems, including engine failure. That's why it's important to have your timing belt inspected and replaced as needed.

Our expert mechanics will conduct a thorough inspection of your timing belt, pulleys, and tensioner. We'll carefully examine the belt for signs of wear, such as fraying, cracking, or excessive stretching. We'll also inspect the pulleys and tensioner for any damage or misalignment. If we find any issues, we'll recommend the necessary repairs.

If your timing belt needs to be replaced, we'll use high-quality replacement parts and ensure that it's installed correctly. We'll carefully remove the old belt and install the new one, paying close attention to the alignment and tension.
